What to control

Uses Windows' own media controls, so it works with YouTube in any browser, YouTube Music, Spotify's desktop app and most other players. Only ever play and pause — your volume is never touched.

How death is detected

Lock In watches for the KILLED BY banner using the OCR engine built into Windows. The text is either on screen or it isn't, so there is nothing to calibrate and nothing to guess.

Hype mode

When it comes down to you against one of them, your hype music kicks in — the one time music plays while you're alive. It stops when the round ends.

Right-click a Kryd playlist or track in Spotify → Share → Copy link and paste it here. A YouTube URL works too, or the path to a folder of MP3s to play from the app itself.

What the scanner sees

Valorant prints how many are alive on each team in the two small boxes beside the round timer. Lock In reads those digits off the screen: a 1 is a thin vertical bar, every other digit is wide. Scan mid-round and the digit it found is outlined.

When you die

The card can't draw over a game in exclusive fullscreen — Windows blocks that for every app. Use borderless, or turn on "Say it out loud".
